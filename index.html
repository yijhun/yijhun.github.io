Unveiling the Physics of Star Formation

My research focuses on the complex dynamics of high-mass star formation regions. By combining radio interferometric observations with numerical simulations, I investigate how massive stars accrete matter and interact with their environments.

Radio Observation of
NGC 7538 IRS1

Investigating the accretion flow around high-mass protostars. I utilize MIRIAD for reducing and analyzing interferometric data, performing self-calibration and imaging to resolve the molecular gas kinematics in the NGC 7538 IRS1 region.

Ulrich Infall Model Simulation

To understand the physical structure of infalling envelopes, I develop Python codes to simulate the Ulrich model. This involves visualizing velocity fields and density distributions to compare theoretical predictions with observational signatures.

Scientific Computing

Proficient in Python for astrophysics (Astropy, NumPy, Matplotlib, SciPy). Experience with GitHub Actions for research workflow automation (e.g., daily arXiv paper tracking) and Linux-based data reduction environments.
